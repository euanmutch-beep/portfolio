My Story & Mission

Growing up in the care system, I faced instability, addiction, depression, and self-doubt. Fitness became my turning point – giving me structure, discipline, and hope when I needed it most.

After years in local government, I took the leap into full-time coaching. With the support of my wife, I qualified as a Level 3 Personal Trainer and continued specialising in areas that matter deeply to me: addiction recovery, mental health, menopause, and supporting older adults.

New Mindset New Power is more than a business – it's a movement. We create safe spaces for transformation through functional fitness, 3D loaded movement, community bootcamps, and personalised coaching. Reset. Rebuild. Rise.
